# Security policy

## Scope

This policy covers the Cyber-Threat.net Hunt Lab repository (<https://github.com/CyberThreatNet/hunt-lab>). That includes its queries, rules, pipelines and GitHub Actions workflows.

**Please report privately if:**
- a query or rule could cause harm when run, for example by being unexpectedly expensive or destructive;
- content in the repository leaks sensitive data;
- there is an issue with our CI or supply chain;
- you find exploit code or unsafe content that should not be here.

**This is not the place to report** vulnerabilities in third-party products such as PHP or Plex. Report those to the vendor, or to the [NCSC](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/information/vulnerability-reporting) or your national CERT.

Missed detections or false positives are not security issues. Please use the [issue forms](https://github.com/CyberThreatNet/hunt-lab/issues/new/choose) for those.

## How to report

**Do not open a public issue.** Use one of these:

- GitHub's private vulnerability reporting: [Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/CyberThreatNet/hunt-lab/security/advisories/new).
- Email **security@cyber-threat.net**.

Please include:
- what you found and where (file and line);
- its impact;
- how to reproduce it safely.

Do not send live malware, credentials or personal data.

## What to expect

- We aim to acknowledge reports within 5 working days.
- We will keep you updated until the issue is resolved.
- We credit reporters who want to be credited.
- Please give us reasonable time to fix an issue before you disclose it publicly.
